💉 What are Skin Boosters?

Evidence-based injectable treatments designed to improve skin quality at a cellular level — not to alter facial structure. Acting as "injectable skincare," formulations are delivered directly into the dermis to stimulate fibroblast activity.

Boosts hydration · collagen · elasticity from within
